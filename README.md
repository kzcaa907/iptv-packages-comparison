## What does “connection” actually mean?

This is one of those small words that can cause a surprisingly large amount of confusion.

On TiviLive, a **connection means one simultaneous stream**. One connection means one screen can stream at a time. Two connections allow two simultaneous screens, three connections allow three, and so on up to five. Installing the service on several devices is not the same thing as streaming on several devices at once.

So the choice is pretty practical:

**1 connection:** sensible for one main viewer or one household screen at a time.

**2 connections:** useful when two people may watch different things simultaneously.

**3–5 connections:** designed for households where multiple screens may be active at the same time.

That means you don't necessarily need the biggest IPTV package just because it exists. If the second TV spends most of its life displaying a screensaver, paying for extra simultaneous connections may not accomplish much.
